**Solana Heat** — an original open-world crime game set in Port Solana, a sun-soaked coastal city invented for this entry: seven-by-five blocks of towers, shops, homes, parks and parking lots, a beach, and the little island of Solana Key across one long bridge. You play Rico Vega, a broke wheelman who takes jobs from fixer Sol Duarte: five story missions (a first car, a hot delivery to the Hotel Marisol, a six-checkpoint run, a lesson in losing the police, and the Banco del Sol job), with free-roam taxi fares once the story is done.

The city lives around you: traffic drives the grid and brakes for pedestrians, people stroll the sidewalks and run from gunfire, and the PSPD answers every crime with a five-star wanted system — Interceptors that chase, ram and pin you until the bust meter fills, unless you break away and lay low. Steal and drive five kinds of cars, shoot on foot with mouse aim, watch the chase unfold on the live minimap, and save your cash and progress between visits. Everything is hand-written vanilla JavaScript and canvas — no engine, no libraries, no assets — with synthesised WebAudio sound (engine, sirens, gunfire, paydays).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# then play it: serve the game/ folder with any static server, e.g.
cd game && python3 -m http.server 8080
# and open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```

Controls: WASD/arrows to move and drive, E to enter or leave a car, mouse and click to aim and shoot, F to take a job at Sol's Garage, T for a taxi fare while driving, P or Esc to pause.
